Age Revolution

BA Final Collection · LCI Barcelona · 2020

P.07 · Independent R&D · v0.1 concept

Pattern
Intelligence

A searchable memory for pattern-making knowledge. Photograph a garment, find the base that already solves it.

Personal concept and independent project. Not a product of, affiliated with, or used by any employer. All data shown is illustrative.

Then I started noticing the system.

Every garment passes through the same chain of decisions. Some need a pattern maker’s judgement. Many are repetitions: finding a base someone made two seasons ago, re-applying a correction made a hundred times, adjusting a length across a whole size range. And a lot of that knowledge lives in people’s memory, where nobody else can search it.

  1. 01Design brief
  2. 02Base searchThe right base exists somewhere. Finding it depends on who remembers it.
  3. 03Pattern
  4. 04Fitting
  5. 05CorrectionsThe same fit fixes, repeated garment after garment.
  6. 06GradingRule-based work still done largely by hand.
  7. 07Production

A generic development flow. Blue notches mark where repetitive work hides.

A The problem

Pattern departments hold an enormous amount of knowledge: bases, previous patterns and the experience of the people who made them. Yet finding the right base, adapting a pattern, grading it or repeating a familiar correction is still mostly manual.

B The idea

Turn that knowledge into a structured library anyone on the team can use. Upload or photograph a garment; the system reads its silhouette, fit, volume, length, structure and construction, compares it with the library of bases and returns the closest starting points, each linked to its pattern and CLO file.

  1. 01CaptureUpload or photograph a garment.
  2. 02AnalyseSilhouette, fit, volume, length, structure, construction.
  3. 03SearchCompare against a library of bases, by meaning, not file name.
  4. 04MatchTop pattern matches, ranked and explained.
  5. 05OpenGo straight to the pattern and the CLO file.
