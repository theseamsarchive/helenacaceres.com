Nina Skarra

Digital Pattern Maker · Remote · 2022 – 2023

Nina Skarra is a luxury fashion designer from Oslo. Her pieces were sold online from 3D renders, before a single one was sewn.

I built each garment in CLO3D and created the renders for the online shop. Once the orders were in, I drafted the production patterns and the pieces were made in London, only in the quantities sold.
